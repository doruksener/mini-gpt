The Project mini-gpt architecture is designed to understand the fundamental mechanics of transformer models. 
In the beginning, there was text. Text is converted into tokens. Tokens are converted into numbers. 
Numbers flow through embedding layers, passing into the multi-head self-attention mechanism. 
Attention is all you need to map relationships between words in a sequence. 
The model learns to predict the next token, step by step, token by token, layer by layer. 
Through backpropagation and gradient descent, the loss decreases, and intelligence emerges from simple matrix multiplications. 
We train the network on this corpus, watching the loss fall from high numbers down to near zero. 
A transformer processes sequences in parallel, using masks to prevent looking into the future. 
Generative pre-training allows the model to complete sentences, write poetry, and generate code. 
This is the core philosophy of deep learning: simple operations scaled up to massive scales. 
Now, let us build the model, write the training loop, and watch the weights update in real-time.
